Big City Roleplay: Real Life Simulator City Game

Urban life like never before: choose your role, master your profession, and explore endless adventures in a living city.

🏛 Part of our origins. This Unity title belongs to the studio's first chapter and has been retired from the stores. The lessons it taught us live on in every iOS app we ship today — see what we're building now.

Experience Real-Life Adventures like Never Before

Prepare yourself for an extraordinary journey into the heart of urban life with Big City Roleplay: Real Life Simulator City Game. Step into a world where you become the hero in a city teeming with opportunities and challenges.

Live Out Thrilling Character-Driven Stories

In this immersive metropolis, you have the freedom to choose from three exhilarating professions, each essential for your survival. Your choices matter as you strive to balance food, drink, and finances to thrive in this lifelike urban playground:

  • Master of Pizza Delivery: 🍕 As a pizza delivery aficionado, your mission is to satisfy the cravings of hungry customers. Follow your boss’s cues to make lightning-fast deliveries. It’s not just a game; it’s a chance to live your dream as a delivery dynamo.
  • Rule the Streets as a Taxi Maestro: 🚖 Take the wheel as a taxi driver, where your sole objective is to safely transport passengers to their destinations. Just like a real cabbie, you’ll receive customer requests, adding authenticity to your role in this epic urban saga.
  • Answer the Call as an Ambulance Ace: 🚑 Ever dreamt of being a hero behind the wheel of an ambulance? In this city life simulator, you can answer the call of duty. Your mission as an ambulance driver is straightforward: reach the patient’s location and deliver them to the hospital on time. Leave the rest to the medical team.

Endless Roleplay Adventures Await

Beyond these thrilling roles, Big City Roleplay offers a plethora of captivating activities in the sprawling metropolis:

  • Cinema Nights: 🎬 Earn and unwind with a night at the movies.
  • Home Sweet Home: 🏡 After a long day, return to your sanctuary to relax and recharge.
  • Race to Glory: 🏎️ Feel the adrenaline rush as you race an F1 car on the Nurbur circuit, accessible via the upper airport road.
  • Car Collector’s Dream: 🚗 Build a garage of your dream cars and explore the city in style.
  • Car Swap Magic: 🔄 Easily exchange cars within this real-life simulator for a fresh driving experience.
  • Reach for the Skies: ✈️ Achieve the ultimate status symbol by owning your very own plane, accessible via the upper airport road.

Key Features That Set Big City Roleplay Apart

  • Seamless Navigation: 🗺️ Explore the city effortlessly with our zoomable, interactive mini-map.
  • Intuitive Interface: ℹ️ Enjoy a user-friendly interface that ensures your gaming experience is smooth and immersive.
  • Stunning Visuals: 🖥️ Dive into a world of mesmerizing graphics that will keep you captivated throughout your adventure.

Don’t miss out on this extraordinary opportunity! Download Big City Roleplay: Real Life Simulator City Game now and embark on an incredible journey where you take on diverse roles in this dynamic urban setting.
