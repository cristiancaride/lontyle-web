Code Tip Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2025

1. Introduction

CodeTip respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the CodeTip mobile application.

By using the app, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

CodeTip does not collect personally identifiable information such as name, email address, phone number, or location.

We may collect limited, non-personal data for app functionality:

Device tokens (used only to send push notifications)

Anonymous usage data (via Firebase, such as app launches or crashes)

This data cannot be used to identify you personally.

3. How We Use Information

The collected data is used only to:

Deliver daily coding tips

Send push notifications when new tips are available

Improve app stability and performance

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Push Notifications

CodeTip may send push notifications to inform you about new daily coding tips.

You can disable notifications at any time from your device settings.

5. Data Storage & Security

All data is transmitted securely using standard system encryption (HTTPS).

We rely on Apple’s iOS security features and Firebase’s infrastructure.

No sensitive personal data is stored on our servers.

6. Third-Party Services

CodeTip uses the following third-party services:

Firebase (Google LLC) for backend services and analytics

These services may collect limited technical data according to their own privacy policies.

7. Children’s Privacy

CodeTip does not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13.

8. Your Rights

You have full control over the app:

You can stop data collection by uninstalling the app

You can disable notifications at any time

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.Any changes will be reflected in this document.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at:
