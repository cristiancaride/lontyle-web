iGarage Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Introduction

At iGarage, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is safeguarded. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and manage user information within our application.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information from users:

  • Workshop name.
  • Phone numbers of clients added in the app.
  • Vehicle license plates.
  • Vehicle make and model.

Use of Information

The collected information is used solely for the workshop to contact the client quickly and efficiently. iGarage does not use this data for any other purpose.

Information Sharing

We do not share, sell, or distribute users’ personal information to third parties. All data is securely stored in Firestore.

Data Security

No specific security measures are implemented beyond storage in Firestore. Information is stored in the cloud, and an annual data cleanup is conducted.

Data Storage

Data is stored in Firestore and reviewed annually. Data that is no longer needed is systematically deleted.

User Rights

Users have no rights to modify their data unless they notify us via email at lontyle@gmail.com.

Changes to the Privacy Policy

Users will not be notified of changes to this policy. Users are encouraged to review this policy periodically.

Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at lontyle@gmail.com.
