How To Play Little City Island Roleplay

Eat and Drink

As a human we all need to drink and eat to keep us alive, this also happens in this game, so to keep your eating and drinking levels, there are some stops in the map.

For drinking you can go to any red machine like that and buy drinks

Save / Change Car

To save or change the car you will have to go to the same location as claiming a car, but you will have to go inside here with your car:

Once the car is INSIDE the garage exit the car, and it will be automatically saved. Now you can claim another car if you want.

JOBS

To get a job you should always go to the Town Hall, here:

Mining

Then you will have to wait for the ship to come, once the ship has arrived just get inside of it.

When the ship has arrived to the another stop you can now exit and you will be in the Mine.

Now to begin Mining just go inside the Mining House, you will see a little circle, just get in there and a panel should show Start Mining and Stop Mining.

To begin mining press Start Mining, then go outside the house and you will have to start looking for (Gold, Silver, Copper) lingots, to take them you just have to stand on them, the money will vary depending on which material you took.

The money will be added automatically to your account when you take a Lingot.

When there is not any more Lingots or you just want to stop mining, go to the Mining House and press Stop Mining.
