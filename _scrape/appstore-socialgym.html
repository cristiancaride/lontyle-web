Thousands of community routines, an AI coach, duels with friends and a monthly XP race. Train guided and keep your streak alive. Social Gym is the social network for people who lift. Find your next routine, train guided with timed rests, and compete with your friends for XP. FIND YOUR ROUTINE • Thousands of community routines, filtered by goal, level, days and equipment • Muscle analysis with an interactive 3D body: see what each routine works before you start • Remix any routine and make it yours TRAIN GUIDED • Sets, reps and timed rests with lock-screen alerts • Set-by-set weight logging and automatic personal records • Apple Health sync (write-only) PROGRESS LIKE A GAME • Earn XP with every workout, level up and protect your weekly streak • Achievements, history with charts and a home-screen widget COMPETE WITH YOUR COMMUNITY • Weekly 1v1 duels: whoever earns more XP wins • Monthly XP race with a podium and champion • The Wall: share your wins with your gym and community AI COACH 24/7 • Training, nutrition and recovery questions answered instantly • AI routine generator tailored to you ELITE PLAN (one-time purchase, no subscription) • Everything unlimited: saves, AI Coach and generator • Advanced analytics, CSV export, pro calculators and zero ads Download Social Gym and train with your community.
